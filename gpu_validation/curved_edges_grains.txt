Curved-boundary physics + grains validation (geometry-composition branch)
labmac04 (Quadro P1000), Julia 1.11.9.

=== Curved-boundary physics: vortex in a disc, staircase vs edge-smoothed (CPU) ===
Permalloy disc, 360 nm diameter, 80x80x4 cells (5 nm), relaxed vortex.
  edgesmooth=0 (staircase): E_total=8.499e-17 J  E_demag=3.17e-18 J
  edgesmooth=4:             E_total=8.278e-17 J  E_demag=9.60e-19 J
  edgesmooth=8:             E_total=8.248e-17 J  E_demag=6.56e-19 J
The demag energy drops ~5x with smoothing: the staircase overcounts the spurious
magnetic surface charge on a jagged curved edge. Total energy decreases and
converges toward the continuum as edgesmooth grows. The vortex relaxes cleanly
(core within ~1.5 nm of centre) in all cases — the expected, correct behaviour.

=== Curved-boundary relax + grains, CPU vs GPU ===
Edge-smoothed disc (edgesmooth=8), relaxed vortex, seed cleared with clearempty!
before togpu:
  CPU <m>=(0,0,0.00152)  GPU <m>=(0,0,0.00152)  |diff|=1.1e-9  state rel diff=7.0e-6
  empty cells |m| = 0 on both.
Polycrystal (Voronoi, 40 nm grains, random anisotropy), effective field:
  CPU vs GPU rel diff = 2.9e-16.

Note: clearempty! is CPU-only. A hand-built setconfig array must be cleared on the
CPU (clearempty!(m, rp)) before togpu — otherwise empty/partial cells carry a
nonzero moment on the GPU and the relax diverges. setmag! on a Simulation already
clears, so the normal workflow is unaffected. (Documented in the manual's GPU
section.)

Conclusion: the geometry-composition branch (phases 1-3: shapes/transforms, edge
smoothing, Voronoi grains) is physically correct on curved boundaries and matches
CPU↔GPU. Ready to merge.
