Relax crosses over around ~65k cells and reaches ~2x on this modest GPU. The
minimizer step (Cayley + two BB reductions) has more kernel launches than the
effective field alone, so its speedup is a bit lower than the ~3x of the field
evaluation; a stronger GPU (Colab) should improve both.
