JuliaMag demag + full effective field on GPU — labmac04 (Quadro P1000)
Julia 1.11.9, CUDA.jl. Repo: main + GPU demag extension.

=== Correctness: demag field, CPU vs GPU (max rel diff) ===
geometry    cells    max rel diff
std4-ish    2500     6.38e-16
cube 3D     32768    6.05e-16
thin film   16384    5.80e-16

=== Correctness: FULL effective field (exchange+anisotropy+demag+zeeman) ===
geometry    cells    max rel diff
std4        2500     3.83e-16
cube 3D     16384    4.14e-16
thin film   16384    4.61e-16

Every term, including the FFT demag convolution (via CUFFT), agrees with the CPU
to floating-point precision. The GPU demag plan is the CPU DemagPlan uploaded to
the device (transformed kernel copied over, CUFFT plans + device scratch built);
pad / pointwise tensor·vector / crop are array operations, no scalar indexing.

=== Performance: full effective field WITH demag, CPU vs GPU (ms/call) ===
cells      CPU(ms)   GPU(ms)   speedup(CPU/GPU)
4096       0.52      1.06      0.49
16384      2.45      2.23      1.10
65536      13.64     7.34      1.86
262144     59.65     27.55     2.17
524288     269.01    87.69     3.07
1048576    326.16    115.32    2.83

The GPU now beats the CPU: crossover at ~16k cells, up to ~3x at 512k cells — on
a modest 640-core Quadro P1000. The demag FFT (the dominant per-step cost) scales
far better on the GPU than the isolated field kernels did (see
gpu_scaling_*.txt). A last-generation GPU (planned Colab benchmark) should widen
this substantially.

Note: the field terms (exchange/anisotropy/demag/zeeman) and effectivefield! run
on the GPU end-to-end via togpu(world). The energy minimizer and the time
integrator still use scalar CPU loops and are the next extension target for a
full relax/run on the GPU.
